Fourth Design Section: UI / Operator Workflow

V4.5 should not create a separate product area at first. Put a compact Recommendations section under Reports / Quality Analytics, with drilldown links back to the evidence. Selecting a recommendation opens a focused review surface showing evidence, rationale, target file/surface, patch preview, risk, and actions.

The default action model is accept, reject, and defer. Accepting means “this recommendation is valid and may produce a patch preview”; it does not mean “apply to disk immediately.”
